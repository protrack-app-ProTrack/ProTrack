# 🎥 Arcade Recording Blueprint — ProTrack Interactive Demo

Since Arcade is a browser-extension based tool, you need to record the clicks on your screen. Follow this blueprint to create a 100% professional interactive product demo in **under 2 minutes**.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Install the Arcade Extension
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to the **Chrome Web Store** and search for **"Arcade - Interactive Demo"**.
3. Click **"Add to Chrome"** to install the extension.
4. Pin the Arcade extension to your browser toolbar (click the puzzle icon and select the pin next to Arcade).

---

## 🎬 Step 2: The Perfect Recording Sequence (Perform these clicks)

Before clicking **"Start Recording"**, open your website link:  
👉 `https://protrack-app-protrack.github.io/ProTrack/`

Now, click the Arcade extension icon and select **"Start Recording"**. Perform this exact sequence of actions:

| Action Number | Screen Area / Element | Action to Take | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Sign In Card | Enter email `aarav@college.edu` and password `student123`, then click **"Sign In"**. | Introduces the Student workspace login. |
| **2** | Top Header | Click the **Light/Dark Toggle** icon (Sun/Moon). | Showcases the custom glassmorphism theme and contrast adaptability. |
| **3** | Navigation Sidebar | Click on **"Task Board"**. | Moves the viewer to the Kanban board. |
| **4** | Kanban Board Toolbar | Click the **"Assignee"** dropdown and choose **"Diya"**. | Demonstrates live quick board filters. |
| **5** | Kanban Board Columns | Drag a task from **"In Progress"** to **"Done"** (confetti will fire). | Shows interactive state transitions. |
| **6** | Navigation Sidebar | Click on **"Timeline"**. | Shows the horizontal Gantt chart roadmap. |
| **7** | Timeline Grid | Click on any task bar in the Gantt grid (details modal opens). | Shows interactive roadmap inspection. |
| **8** | Navigation Sidebar | Click on **"Analytics"**. | Opens the burndown line chart widgets. |
| **9** | Navigation Sidebar | Click on **"My Team"**. | Navigates to peer reviews. |
| **10** | Team Ratings | Click a star to rate a teammate and click **"Submit Ratings"**. | Shows peer rating submission. |
| **11** | User Profile Panel | Click **"Logout"** (bottom of sidebar). | Clears session. |
| **12** | Sign In Card | Log in as **`sharma@college.edu`** (password: `faculty123`). | Switches to Faculty supervisor role. |

## 🏁 Step 3: Publish and Share
1. Click the blinking **Arcade extension icon** in your toolbar to **"Stop Recording"**.
2. A new tab will open in Arcade showing your interactive preview.
3. **Add Tooltips (Callouts):** In the editor, you can add text annotations on each slide (e.g. *"Click here to view Gantt Timeline"*).
4. Click **"Publish"** and copy the shareable link to email to your mentor!
